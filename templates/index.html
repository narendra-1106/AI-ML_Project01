Academic AI/ML Methodology Note

To avoid temporal data leakage, our machine learning pipeline strictly uses pre-match features only (teams, toss, and venue) and performs chronological train/test splitting. Post-match information like match margin, score totals, or player of the match are strictly excluded from prediction inputs.
